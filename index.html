How Statpad works

Every number on the site comes from the four steps below.

01 · FETCH

Results, fixtures, prices

Results and fixtures from football-data.co.uk and ESPN; bookmaker prices from The Odds API and, through ESPN, DraftKings. Women's football and more of the world are covered too.

02 · RATE

Attack & defence

A Poisson model with home advantage gives every team an attack and defence rating from goals and shots on target. Recent games count more, and small samples are pulled towards average.

03 · BLEND

Model meets market

The bookmakers' consensus is stripped of margin by the power method, with Pinnacle weighted up. Expected goals are fitted to it and blended with the model, the market taking the larger share.

04 · PRICE

Every market

A Dixon–Coles scoreline grid gives every market. Value is the best real price against the bookmakers' own fair price, and slips are optimised for hit chance.
