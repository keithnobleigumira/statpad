How Statpad works

Every number on the site comes from the four steps below.

01 · FETCH

Results, fixtures, prices

Results and fixtures from football-data.co.uk; fixtures and bookmaker prices for leagues, European competitions and cups from The Odds API.

02 · RATE

Attack & defence

A Poisson model with home advantage gives every team an attack and defence rating. Recent games count more, and small samples are pulled towards average.

03 · BLEND

Model meets market

The bookmakers' consensus is stripped of margin, with sharp books weighted up. Expected goals are fitted to it and blended 80/20 with the model.

04 · PRICE

Every market

A Dixon–Coles scoreline grid gives every market. Edge is measured against the best real price, and slips are optimised for hit chance.
